Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026 · pacificislandventures.com

The short version

This website doesn't collect your personal information. It has no accounts, no forms, no analytics, no ad tags, and sets no cookies of its own. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. The Space Traders game has its own privacy policy, linked below. Earlier this year we also ran a closed-beta signup, which is now closed; people who signed up will find what we hold, and how to have it removed, further down.

This website

What we collect: nothing from visitors. This is a static website. We run no analytics, no advertising or conversion tags, and no trackers, and we don't set cookies.

Third parties: pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google, which can see your IP address and browser details when it does. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical details such as your IP address when your browser loads a page. We don't receive that data. Both are covered by their own policies: Google's privacy policy and GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email us: we receive your email address and what you send us, and we use it to reply to you.

The Space Traders game

The game is covered by its own policy, which explains what the app does and doesn't collect: Space Traders privacy policy. This page covers the website only.

The closed-beta signup (closed)

Until October 14, 2026, this site had a page where you could sign up for the Space Traders closed beta with "Sign in with Google". That page has been removed.

If you signed up: Google sent us a signed token containing your email address, your name, and a Google account identifier. We stored these, with a timestamp, in a private spreadsheet. We use them only to manage the beta list and to email you about the beta from play@pacificislandventures.com, and for nothing else.

Ad measurement: while the signup page was up, it included Google's conversion measurement tag so that Google could report how many signups came from our ads. The tag has been removed. Any cookies Google set while it was live are governed by Google's privacy policy, and you can control how Google uses ad data at myadcenter.google.com.

What we don't do

We never sell, share, or trade your email address or name with anyone.

Coming off the list

If you signed up for the beta, email play@pacificislandventures.com from the address you signed up with and ask to be removed. We'll delete your row and stop emailing you.

Changes to this policy

If we change how this site handles your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: play@pacificislandventures.com.
