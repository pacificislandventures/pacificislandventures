Privacy Policy

Last updated September 1, 2026 · pacificislandventures.com

The short version

This site collects one thing, in one place: if you sign up for the Space Traders closed beta with your Google account, we receive the email address and name on that account. We use them to send you beta invites and build announcements, and for nothing else. The rest of the site collects nothing.

What we collect and why

Beta signup (/beta/): signing up uses Google's "Sign in with Google". Google sends us a signed token containing your email address, your name, and a Google account identifier. We store these, with a timestamp, in a private spreadsheet. We use them solely to manage the closed beta list and email you about the beta from play@pacificislandventures.com.

Everything else: this is a static website. We run no analytics, set no cookies of our own, and use no trackers. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and the beta page loads Google's sign-in script; those requests go to Google and are covered by Google's privacy policy.

What we don't do

We never sell, share, or trade your email address or name with anyone. We don't use them for marketing beyond the beta itself, and we don't combine them with data from anywhere else.

Coming off the list

Email play@pacificislandventures.com from the address you signed up with and ask to be removed. We'll delete your row and stop emailing you.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: play@pacificislandventures.com.
