Space Traders is a single-player space trading game. Start with one small ship and a thin ledger, and work your way up to a fleet, a crew, and stations of your own — across a galaxy that's generated fresh for every captain.

Sign up for the closed beta

What you'll be doing

No ads, no energy timers, no pay-to-win. Just systems that push back and a ledger that keeps score.

🪐 A living economy

Every station has its own character — industry, appetite, and stock that moves. Prices respond to events, shortages, and what you haul through the door. Reading the market is the game.

🛠️ Ships worth earning

A deep ship tree across distinct hull families. Fit and fly any hull from the berth, refit your fleet, and tune mounts and cores that pull in different directions — power is always a trade-off.

📜 Contracts & colonies

Take colony supply contracts with real deadlines, run courses from planetside boards, and earn delivery bonuses for flying sharp.

🏴‍☠️ Pirates with agendas

Hunters work the same space you do. Fight, flee, or out-plan them — and when you're flush enough, do a little hunting yourself.

🏗️ Build your own stations

Plant stations where the routes say one should exist, and turn your trade knowledge into standing infrastructure.

👥 A crew with faces

Crew aren't stat blocks — they have names, faces, and allegiances that matter when things get tense.
