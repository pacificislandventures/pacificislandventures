5Online features

Some parts of the game use the internet. On the effective date, the online feature is Hunter Packs. You can upload copies of your ships, up to 5 ships in each of 3 packs. Other players can then hunt those copies in their games.

When another player defeats one of your uploaded ships, you earn a reward that you collect in the game. On the effective date, each kill pays 1,000,000 credits and 1 obol.

  • What the game uploads. Copies of your ships: the hull, colours, weapons, upgrades, pilot level and a pilot name that the game makes up. The game does not upload your captain name or any other text you type.

  • Your uploads. When you upload ships, you let us store the copies and show them to other players in the game while the feature runs.
  • Changes. Online features need an internet connection and may not always be available. We may change, limit or stop any online feature, including its rewards. If a feature stops, uploaded ships and rewards you have not collected may be lost.
