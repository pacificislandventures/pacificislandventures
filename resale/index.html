Selling to us

Four steps, no games. Most deals are done inside a few days.

Get in touch

Email contact@pacificislandventures.com with what you've got — photos help, but "three tables of cards and a garage" is a fine start.

We take a look

We assess in person or from photos. Volume doesn't scare us — big lots are the job, not the exception.

One straight offer

A single number for the lot, explained. Take it or don't — no pressure, no drip-feed haggling.

We handle the rest

We pack, we haul, we're gone. Estates and big collections cleared without you lifting a box.

Where we sell

Our stock runs through a local online auction site — cards, collections, estate finds and everything between. New lots list regularly, and everything starts at a real opening bid.
